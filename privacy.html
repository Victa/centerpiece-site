Privacy Policy

Centerpiece – Label Cutter · Last updated October 10, 2026

Centerpiece does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No advertising and no tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any server.

Photos

Photos you take or import are processed entirely on your device to find and cut out the record label. They are never uploaded.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to photograph your record label.
  • Photo library: used only to import a photo you choose, and to save an exported label when you ask to save it.

Exports

Exported PNGs are saved to your photo library or shared through the iOS share sheet only when you request it. Once you share a file with another app or service, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Apple's privacy manifest

The app's privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types, consistent with this policy.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
